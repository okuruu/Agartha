# SECRET LORE (author only)

> **Spoilers. Author-only.** Nothing in this file goes into the chapters, the BIBLE, or `Ideas/` until the author explicitly says to reveal it. Use it only to keep new writing consistent and to avoid contradicting it.
> Collected from the author's answers, Sept 2026. Where something is marked **secret** or **open**, the author has not decided or has not shared it. Don't guess on the page.

---

## 1. The world: Agartha
- **The world of the novel is Agartha.** It lies **inside a hollow Earth**, on the planet's inner surface.
- **Why it exists:** Agartha is **the Earth's heart**, where the planet's life force actually lives. The surface is only the skin; Agartha is the body that keeps it alive.
- **The sky:** a glowing "crust-sky." What Agarthans call the sky is the far side of the world, seen from very far away through haze. The "stars" are distant cities or veins of light.
- **Agarthans don't know** they live inside a hollow world. To them it's just "the sky." At most, a few scholars wonder why the stars never move.
- **Who knows the two worlds exist:** only the powerful. Rulers and the organizations draining the earth-power know on both sides, and keep it secret.
- **Time runs the same** in Agartha and on the surface. A year below is a year above.

## 2. The world is splitting apart
- Drought, earthquakes, famine, bad politicians, war: everything is being ground to dust. **It is not an apocalypse.** It is very bad, but **recoverable**.
- **Both literal and figurative, starting now.** Cracks are just beginning, and most people blame earthquakes. It becomes visibly literal later in the saga, as continents pull apart and their Pulses weaken.

## 3. The Pulses (the earth-power)
- **The Earth has a literal heart.** A great chamber beneath each continent **beats**, and the power *is* that heartbeat.
- **7 great Pulses**, one per continent, mirroring the surface, **plus a few lesser ones** under large islands. Some of those are forgotten or already bled dry.
- **Draining a Pulse bleeds the planet.** The beat weakens, and far-off land goes dry, shakes, and starves, the way a hand goes cold.
- **The surface feels it too.** People up there know nothing about it; they only notice that fires, droughts, and earthquakes are happening more often.
- **How they're drained:** **secret.** The drainer is unknown.
- **Who drains this Kingdom's Pulse:** **open.**
- **Where this Kingdom's Pulse is, and who holds it:** **open.**
- **What the drainers want:** **long life, escape, and absolute control.** "Escape" means **out of Agartha**: the drainers are *from* Agartha and want to rise to the surface.
- **Not related to the Pulses:** the dragons, the gold oil in Aldric's tonic, and (as far as decided) the Titans.

## 4. Esdel
- **"Esdel" is an alias**, not his real name, but he believes it is his name. His real name will be revealed later.
- **He is a man from the surface who fell into Agartha by accident.** Others found him and used him.
- **Whether the drain caused his fall:** **secret.**
- **Before the fall:** IQ around 125, master's-degree-level thesis work, INTJ-A. He loves to learn and study, and still works on high-school math problems for fun. He likes to make jokes, but is very careful with the other person's feelings. Not very introverted; he likes making friends. He talks in a way that makes anyone feel comforted, which sometimes draws women's attention; he usually senses it and steps aside.
- This is why he knows "physics," neuroscience, and lab method, and writes in a hand nobody recognizes. **Never explain this on the page.**
- **He never lies** (this rule is also in the BIBLE).

## 5. The Karunākṣa and the Kālākṣa
- **Revised Oct 2026: the Karunākṣa is simply red, fox-slit-pupil eyes.** They let him take in and process far more information than a normal mind can. The cost is **brain overload**: headaches, nausea, lost words, confusion, and with extreme overuse, days of it. **Recoverable, painful, annoying.** No bleeding eyes, no ruptured veins.
- **Open:** whether the eyes were implanted (earlier idea: he wasn't born with them, which would explain why the overload hits so hard) or are a trait of the Kālākṣa. Don't state either on the page.
- **The Kālākṣa** are the clan that can use the Karunākṣa. It is a gift, but only a few could use it to the full.
- **It is a battle eye, and not more than that.** At full power it predicts even a wrist's movement, giving a huge advantage even when outmatched. **Paired with battle IQ, unmatched.** It does **not** see life spans, souls, or the Earth's heart. (The "healer who sees how long a heart has left" is only a legend in the Kingdom, Ch68.)

## 6. Other decisions
- **The enemy nation** (dark armor, crimson insignia, Titans) is a **rival on the same continent**, sharing this region's Pulse.
- **Dragons** have nothing to do with the Pulses. They are majestic, grand creatures in their own right.
- **Titans:** **open.**
- **The gold oil / Aldric's secret:** its own mystery, unrelated to the Pulses. The tonic binds the drinker to **whoever keeps them company while it works**, not to the pourer (this is also in the BIBLE).
