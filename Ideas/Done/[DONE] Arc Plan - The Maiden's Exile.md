# Arc Plan: *The Maiden's Exile* (Ch67–98)

**Status:** DONE. Written in full as Ch67–98.
**Source outline:** `Next Arc.md` items 12–23. Items 1–11 are already written (Ch51–66). Where the novel differs from the outline, the novel wins (BIBLE §7).
**Length:** 32 chapters.
**Season:** spring into early summer, Year 2. Peach blossoms in the Lián garden. In Edenridge it's one year since Esdel arrived.

---

## Core design

**What the arc is about:** Xueyao comes home to rescue her mother and loses everything left: her mother, her brother, her name. Esdel comes along as an "expendable" apothecary and ends up choosing exile. That choice means he can never go back to Edenridge. His promise to Lyora ("I'll come back") becomes something he can only keep by staying away.

**Why Esdel follows Xueyao into exile (it has to be earned):**
1. He analyzed the tonic and testified, so Aldric knows his name.
2. Aldric has read Esdel's levy record, knows he came from **Edenridge**, and hints at it (Ch76). If Esdel stays, he dies. If he goes home, he brings the hunt to Lyora's door.
3. There's a child (Yueming) and a friend (Xueyao) he won't abandon. Mercy is who he is (*Karunākṣa*, the eye of compassion).

**Why Aldric wants Yueming and Xueyao:** the Heavenly Dragon bloodline is in **both** Lián children. Guardianship of Yueming plus marriage to Xueyao would give Aldric the dragon's leash for a generation. So hiding Yueming isn't only love. It keeps the dragon-blood out of Aldric's hands.

**The King's "ripples" (thread 13), hinted and not confirmed:** he let Lián fall, left Reng in debt, and now lets Aldric reach for the dragon-blood. A tool he can break later ("until they aren't") will hand the dragon to the Crown.

**Why Xueyao's leave was suddenly granted (thread 16), resolved here:** **Aldric** arranged it through his new influence. He wanted her in the capital, away from her army and her dragon. Rowan had wanted her kept at the front, and this is the first crack between them.

**The dragon:** stays at North Ridge. She never calls it in this arc, because it would feed on souls. That restraint is her tragedy and her dignity. At the end, rumor says the dragon sleeps, waiting for her call (Next Arc §23).

**Esdel never tells Xueyao "the rest"** about the tonic: that her mother's love for Aldric was brewed. (Next Arc §13; he carries it into exile.)

**Nobody is simplified:**
- Aldric is charming, patient, and frightening, never a cartoon.
- Rowan is cold and calculating, and quietly lets the Maiden run because "a Reng with a dragon is worse than a traitor without one."
- Ardbert is kind but bound. He can't stand beside her in public, but he opens a gate in private.

## Chapter plan

### Part VI: The Return (Ch67–74)
- **67 The Road South:** five days on the road. Fifteen guards, Changli, Esdel. He treats blisters, and the guards warm to him. Xueyao watches him. At night he writes letters to Lyora that he can't send. Xueyao sees the necklace.
- **68 The Merciful Eye:** a night fire. "Who are you?" He tells her about the desert. She gives a *little*: Karunākṣa means "the eye of compassion." Its bearers could see how long a heart had left. The House of Kālākṣa was ended because "the dragons remember them. Mine does. It hates them." She wonders what her dragon would do if it saw him.
- **69 Serpent over the Gate:** the capital. The Reng serpent hangs over the Lián gate. Aldric's perfect courtesy. Anlan is soft, pliant, and tired, and glows at Aldric. The peach tree is in bloom. **Xueyao meets Yueming for the first time.**
- **70 Interlude: One Year:** Edenridge in spring. A year since the caravan brought him. The lamp is still lit. Eldric's father is walking without a cane. There have been no letters. A passing soldier says the apothecary was "transferred north, alive last we heard."
- **71 The Amber Hour:** each dusk Aldric pours the drops into Anlan's tea himself. Changli steals a vial. Esdel's makeshift laboratory: evaporation, solubility, reagents, and mice.
- **72 What the Mice Knew:** the findings are **greyveil root** (a mild paralytic that slowly weakens heart and will) and an **unknown compound** (the mice bond to whichever hand feeds them the dose). He tells Xueyao about the poison and **withholds the rest.** Changli sees him hesitate.
- **73 A Banquet for the Victor:** a court banquet for the Titan victory. Ardbert and Xueyao have their first real conversation. Aldric watches. Rowan and Aldric exchange a cold word about who got her leave granted.
- **74 The Accusation:** Xueyao accuses Aldric in front of the court. Aldric puts on a wounded show. **Anlan defends him** ("Xueyao, how could you?"). The whispers start: *the general has lost her mind.*

### Part VII: The Unraveling (Ch75–86)
- **75 A Hearing Without Truth:** the royal physicians under Morrell. Esdel testifies and is dismissed as a nameless field apothecary. "A general may command soldiers, but not truth." Ardbert starts to speak, and the King's glance silences him.
- **76 A Pretty Name:** Aldric visits Esdel. He's charming, offers a post and gold, then says: "Edenridge. What a pretty name." Esdel refuses politely, and privately he is terrified.
- **77 The Counter-Draught:** with Xueyao's consent, Esdel brews a withdrawal remedy and Changli swaps the vials. Anlan has tremors and fever.
- **78 Yaoyao:** a lucid dawn. Anlan touches Hengzhi's orchids and remembers. "Yaoyao." Mother and daughter. "I can't remember why I married him." She asks Xueyao to keep Yueming safe.
- **79 The Guardian Question:** rumor that the King will appoint Aldric as Yueming's guardian. Aldric meets the King privately and the Maiden is mentioned. Rowan warns Aldric he is overreaching.
- **80 In Her Sleep:** Anlan dies in the night. Esdel reads the signs: the paralytic was doubled and her breathing stopped. Aldric noticed her waking. **The cure made her dangerous to him.** Esdel's guilt.
- **81 Grief as Art:** a lavish funeral and Aldric's beautiful mourning. Xueyao stands like stone. The King grants Aldric full guardianship.
- **82 Interlude: The Go-Between:** Edenridge in early summer. Eldric's mother presses again after a year. Eldric asks Lyora gently, and she says she is waiting. "Then I'll wait a little too." Arwen's hands have begun to tremble.
- **83 The Proposal:** Aldric proposes to Xueyao, his stepdaughter, framing it as protection ("a woman branded unstable needs a husband's name"). The real aim is dragon-blood heirs. It's implied the King has blessed it. Her answer is ice.
- **84 Under the Peach Tree:** Xueyao breaks, in silence. The temptation to call the dragon and burn the city: "I have fed it men. I can feed it him." Esdel talks her back with mercy. She learns about Lyora. "Then I'll leave." The blood is in Yueming too.
- **85 Knots:** the plan. A sleeping draught for the household, the nursery at midnight, Changli taking Yueming by the river road. Esdel knows he can't go home. He writes Lyora a letter and burns it, because a letter from a traitor would doom her.
- **86 Interlude: The Duke's Arithmetic:** Rowan learns of the proposal. "A Reng with a dragon is worse than a traitor without one." He quietly thins the north watch.

### Part VIII: The Flight (Ch87–98)
- **87 The Storm-Dark Night:** the draught at supper. The nursery. Xueyao holds her brother. "Raise him far from this city. Let him live free of our name." Changli and Xueyao say goodbye.
- **88 The River Road:** Changli, Suyin, and the baby leave by lantern. Xueyao watches until it's gone. Esdel beside her, silent.
- **89 The Gate:** Aldric didn't drink. The alarm is raised. They fight out of the estate. Esdel uses the Veil Form. **Han Ruo holds the gate** and dies.
- **90 Blue Eyes at the North Gate:** Ardbert and Khalid at the thinned north gate. He lies to the watch: "Crown's orders." To Xueyao: "I remember how you looked at people." She passes.
- **91 The Blue Vial:** pursuit to a river ford. Esdel is wounded and uses **Halden's blue stimulant** (thread 11 resolved). The full Karunākṣa, at a cost. They escape.
- **92 The Decree:** the court declares General Lián Xueyao a traitor. The list names "*the apothecary Esdel, late of the village of Edenridge*." Aldric performs outrage. Rowan is silent. Ardbert is pale.
- **93 Nowhere to Return:** the hills. Seven survivors. A trader brings news of the decree. Esdel understands he can never go home. Xueyao offers to release him, and he stays so the hunt never turns south toward Edenridge.
- **94 Interlude: The Backwater Genius:** at the Eastern Post, Halden reads the decree. "Idiot." Benn is silent. At North Ridge, the Imperial Dragon wakes restless, and the war turns.
- **95 Interlude: The Crier:** a royal herald reads the traitors' list in Edenridge square. Esdel's name. Silence. Two men in grey take a room at the inn. Lyora walks home and lights the lamp.
- **96 White on the Ridge:** the borderlands. Xueyao in white mourning at dawn, her hair cut short. For a breath Esdel sees the woman in white from his desert visions, and then it's only Xueyao. (Thread 1 stays open.) "Who are you?" "Esdel. For now that's enough."
- **97 What the Kingdom Says:** legends spread. She wanders the borderlands, and the dragon sleeps beneath her exile. The King with a map. Aldric beside an empty cradle. Ardbert at the north gate at night.
- **98 The Knot:** at a border market, Esdel ties a length of cord in *his* knot and pays a south-bound peddler: "Edenridge. The apothecary. The woman with green eyes. Say nothing." Weeks later, Lyora is handed a cord with no letter. She knows the knot. She ties it to the lamp at the foot of the stairs. **The end of the arc.**

## Guardrails
- Esdel stays faithful to Lyora in heart. There's no romance with Xueyao or Changli. Xueyao may feel something and never say it.
- The woman in white, the crimson-eyed man, the mural, and Esdel's forgotten past stay open.
- Keep Karunākṣa rules: the Veil Form comes from a deliberate blink and ruptures the eyes. The full form comes under mortal pain. He heals.
- The dragon is never summoned.
- Keep style: Lyrical Melancholy Realism, no bold in prose, `---` scene breaks.
