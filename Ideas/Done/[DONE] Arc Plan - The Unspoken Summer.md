## 1. Why this slot

- **Ch22:** Eldric takes Lyora's hand at the festival, and she tries to slip free. The triangle is already there.
- **Ch24:** Esdel breaks down and the three of them hold each other. After a night like that, the next days are tender and awkward, and nobody knows what they are to each other now.
- **Ch25** already opens with "*the quiet, heavy air of the past days had lifted completely.*" This arc becomes those heavy days.
- **Ch27** already contains the payoff line: "*A love that didn't need to be confessed. Because it was already understood.*" It also contains the tease "*Don't burn the tea again like last time!*", and this arc supplies that "last time."

## 2. The heart of it: three good people, no villain

The pain doesn't come from anyone doing wrong. It comes from each of them being right in their own way.

### Esdel: *"I have nothing to give her but a man who might vanish."*
- He has no past, no name beyond one word, and a body that heals in ways it shouldn't (Ch20–21). The nightmare (Ch24) proved that something is still pulling at him.
- Eldric is everything Esdel isn't: rooted, known, and sure of tomorrow.
- **His flaw is noble self-erasure.** He decides for Lyora that she deserves the safe life, and never asks her.
- **Jealousy, and shame about the jealousy.** He can't hate Eldric, because Eldric is kind to him. So the jealousy turns inward.

### Lyora: *"The boy I once wanted is finally looking at me, and I'm looking at someone else."*
- Eldric was her first love (Ch17). She gave up on him because "a lot of girls liked him." Now he has turned toward her. The thing she once wanted most has arrived just after her heart moved on, and part of her grieves that younger self.
- She fears loss above everything (her parents never came back, which is revealed in Ch32). Eldric would stay. Esdel might not.
- **Her jealousy is small and sudden.** Esdel is *not* close to other women. It only takes seeing him *talk* to one and hearing that woman laugh. She's angry at herself for feeling it, and that anger leaks out sideways.

### Eldric: *"I'm a good man, and being good isn't enough."*
- He is **not** a rival in the villain sense. He is warm, honest, and generous, and he genuinely likes Esdel.
- **His situation:** in spring his father's leg was crushed under an overturned cart. It's **healing, but slowly**, from bedridden, to crutch, to cane over the course of the arc. Lyora has been bringing medicine and feverfew. Until his father can work again, Eldric carries the fields alone. His mother, worn thin, wants him married before the harvest so the house has another pair of hands and another heart. The village expects it.
- He has admired Lyora since they were children (Ch18). Lately he has decided to be brave about it. Grabbing her hand at the festival was that bravery, clumsy and sincere.
- **His tragedy:** he does everything right. He asks honestly, and he steps aside gracefully. That grace is what makes it hurt.

> **Rule for writing Eldric:** he never schemes, sneers, or manipulates. Every scene with him should leave the reader thinking *"he deserves someone too."*

## 3. Chapter-by-chapter

### New Ch25: *The Well, and the Rack*
**Beat:** tenderness after the nightmare, then the rumor, then the second-lead heartbreak of building another man's gift.

**Part one: the well**
- The days after Ch24 are gentle and awkward. Lyora sets his tea down a little closer than before, and Esdel notices every small thing.
- At the well (the same well as Ch9, "*it feels like he's always been here*"), Esdel overhears two aunties. Eldric's mother means to send the go-between to Arwen **after the harvest**. "Such a good match. He's carried that farm alone since his father's leg. And Lyora's been bringing them medicine every week."
- Nobody in the rumor asks what Lyora wants.
- Esdel carries the water home without spilling a drop, which is the only sign that anything is wrong. **He starts withdrawing:** he works late at Reyn's, eats after the others have finished, and is always "busy."

**Part two: the rack**
- A few days later, Eldric comes to the workshop, earnest and a little embarrassed. Lyora's old drying rack collapsed under a summer's worth of feverfew. "You're good with your hands. Mine only know dirt. Help me build her a new one?"
- While they work, Eldric opens up: his father trying to stand on the bad leg too soon, his mother's tired hands, and the fear that he'll never see past the southern fields. "*She's the only girl who never looked at me like I was the prize at the fair.*"
- He also apologizes for grabbing her hand at the festival. "I think I frightened her." He's good even in that.
- **Esdel builds it with care he can't hide.** He uses the same bamboo binding knots that hold the irrigation channels (Ch10), his signature without meaning to be.
- Reyn watches from the doorway: "*You're building the door you mean to walk out of.*" Esdel doesn't answer.
- Eldric gives Lyora the rack while Esdel isn't there. She thanks him warmly.
- **Last image:** alone, Lyora runs her thumb over the bindings and stops. She knows those knots. She says nothing. That night she leaves his supper covered by the stairs. In the morning it's still there, cold.

### New Ch26: *A Word in the Market*
**Beat:** the jealousy flips to Lyora, briefly, sharply, and with some humor. Esdel has done nothing wrong.
- A traveling dye-merchant, a sun-browned woman passing through for market day, **never seen again**, asks Esdel why her cart wheel pulls to one side. He answers in his dry, earnest way ("uneven load on the axle"). She laughs and touches his forearm in thanks, and that's all. It lasts thirty seconds. She is gone by evening.
- Lyora sees it from across the square: the laugh, the hand. Something ugly and small flares in her, and she's ashamed of it at once.
- That evening she's clipped with him. "You were busy today." He honestly doesn't understand.
- **She burns the tea**, forgetting the water because she was glaring at the kettle. This is the "last time" from Ch27.
- Arwen hides a smile behind her cup. Later she takes Esdel to prune the garden: "*You cut back what grows wild so the root can hold. But cut too much, and you only have a stick.*" She's talking about his withdrawal, and he half hears it.
- **The ache:** late that night Esdel realizes Lyora was jealous. It should make him happy, but it hurts, because he has already decided to step back.
- **Last image:** each of them lies awake on their own floor, listening to the other not sleep.

### New Ch27: *Eldric's Question*
**Beat:** the honest question, the noble lie, and the overheard fragment.
- At the edge of the southern fields at dusk, Eldric asks straight out, because that's who he is: "Is there something between you and Lyora? Tell me, and I'll step back. I mean that."
- Esdel gives the answer he believes is the kind one: "**No. There's nothing I can offer her.**" It's true in his head and a lie in his heart.
- Eldric believes him, and is relieved and guilty about being relieved. He clasps Esdel's shoulder. "You're a good friend." Esdel feels that like a knife.
- **The overhear:** Lyora is coming up the path with a salve for Eldric's father, who took his first steps on a crutch that morning. She stops behind the hedgerow. She hears only "*No… There's nothing…*" and turns back before the rest.
- **Last image:** Lyora at home grinding herbs far too hard. Arwen quietly takes the pestle from her hands and gives her a cup of tea instead. Neither of them speaks.

### New Ch28: *Rain on the Ridge*
**Beat:** the storm and the piggyback ride. Bodies say what the mouths won't.
- A violent summer storm. Lyora went up the ridge path for feverfew, which is **for Eldric's father**. She's generous even while she's hurting. She twists her ankle on wet roots and can't get down.
- She's missing at dusk. Both men go out searching: Eldric with a lantern and the village, Esdel alone and on instinct. His body finds the path the way it will later read the ambush (a quiet foreshadow; no Karunākṣa).
- Esdel finds her soaked and shivering, and there's no talk. He turns and kneels, and she climbs onto his back.
- **The piggyback:** her cheek on his shoulder and his heartbeat under her palm. It's the heartbeat he fears is "borrowed" (Ch19). To her it feels steady.
- Halfway down they meet Eldric's lantern. Eldric offers to take her, since he's stronger and knows the path. Lyora's fingers **tighten on Esdel's collar**. It's a tiny movement, and Eldric sees it.
- Eldric understands without a word. He smiles, steps aside, and **walks ahead holding the lantern for them the whole way home**, lighting the path for the man she chose. This is his most beautiful scene.
- Fever that night. In her sleep, Lyora murmurs **Esdel's name**. He stays by her until the grey hour and leaves before she wakes. Arwen sees him go and says nothing.

### New Ch29: *The Stairs*
**Beat:** Lyora finally speaks her feelings, all of them except the one. The deepest thing stays unsaid, and both of them understand it.

**Part one: Eldric lets go (gently)**
- A few days later, Lyora's ankle is wrapped. Eldric visits with a basket of peaches (it's summer). His father is **walking with a cane now**, slowly getting better. Eldric talks with Lyora alone for a moment, and we only hear her last line: "*Thank you for the rack. Tell the one who tied the bindings they're perfect.*" Eldric laughs softly. He knows now that she always knew.
- At the gate, Eldric tells Esdel, without anger and with wry sadness: "You lied to me, you know. Badly." Then: "I asked my mother to wait. Not this harvest." He doesn't throw his feelings away. He just sets them down gently. (His thread stays open.)

**Part two: the stairs (the conversation)**
- Night, and soft rain. Neither can sleep. Lyora sits on the lower steps of the stairs between her floor and his attic, her wrapped ankle stretched out. Esdel comes down and sits a few steps above her.
- For a while there's only the rain. Then **Lyora talks.** She's quiet and halting, and she's saying it because the dark makes it easier. She's honest about everything except the word she's afraid of:
  - **The overheard words:** "I heard you. At the fields. '*There's nothing.*'" She doesn't ask him to explain. She says what it did to her: "I told myself it didn't matter. Then I burned the tea again."
  - **Eldric:** "When I was small, I would have given anything for him to look at me the way he does now. And now he does, and all I feel is… sorry. For him. For the girl I was. She waited so long, and I let her down."
  - **The market:** she admits it with a small, embarrassed laugh. "That woman with the cart. I hated her for a whole afternoon. She only asked about a wheel." A pause. "I've never been like that. I don't like who I was that day."
  - **Her fear**, told sideways and without the full story of her parents (that stays in Ch32): "People leave this house, Esdel. They say they'll come back." She stops there.
  - **The rack:** "I knew the knots the moment I touched them. You tie them like you're afraid the water will get away."
- **Esdel mostly listens.** When he speaks, it's short. He doesn't defend the lie. He says once, very low, "*It wasn't nothing.*" Nothing more, and it's enough.
- **She never says "love,"** and he never says it either. At the edge of it, Lyora goes quiet, and the silence finishes the sentence for her. Both of them hear it.
- Then she leans her **head against his knee**. That's the only touch. He doesn't move, so he won't disturb it. They listen to the rain until it stops.
- **The rituals that replace the last words:**
  - From that night on, Lyora leaves **a lamp lit at the foot of the stairs**.
  - Esdel quietly fixes **the creaking step** she always stepped over so she wouldn't wake him.
  - The next morning there are **two cups of tea** on the table. His is made the way *she* takes hers.
  - He eats the supper she leaves, every time.
- **Closing line (a direction, not final):** *She had said everything but one thing, and he had heard that one most clearly of all. The lamp stayed lit. The step no longer creaked.*
- It flows straight into old Ch25's laughing village day: "*the quiet, heavy air of the past days had lifted completely.*"

## 5. Continuity guardrails

- **No spoken promises.** Esdel's first promises come in Ch31–32 (old numbering).
- **Nobody says "love."** Lyora speaks her feelings in Ch29 (new), but the word itself first appears in old Ch29, "*for making me love you this much*." That confession must still land as the first.
- **Esdel isn't close with any woman.** The market scene is a single passing exchange with a stranger. The jealousy is Lyora's reaction, not Esdel's behavior.
- **Eldric stays good and stays open.** He sets his feelings down; he doesn't discard them (bible §6, thread 17).
- **Eldric's father heals slowly and stays alive.** Bedridden, then crutch (Ch27), then cane (Ch29).
- **Physical limit on the stairs:** only her head against his knee.
- **No injuries to Esdel.** That would reveal his overnight healing too early (Ch20–21 is enough).
- **No woman in white, no Karunākṣa.** At most, a hint of instinct in the storm search.
- **Summer** throughout: peaches, harvest coming, storms, cicadas.
- **Lyora's parents aren't explained yet.** That stays in old Ch32. On the stairs she hints ("people leave this house") and stops.
- Arwen doesn't lecture. She speaks in gardening metaphors and silences.
- **Lyora's ankle** should still be mentioned as nearly healed in the next chapter (old Ch25 has her walking the hill, so give it about a week).
