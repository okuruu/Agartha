# Arc Plan: The Apprentice (revision of Ch5–31)

**Status: OPEN.** A revision pass on existing chapters, not new chapters. No renumbering.

## Goal
Before the soldiers come (Ch32), Esdel should read as **Arwen's apprentice first**, with the village's helper and engineer second. Every day starts at the shop: sorting, grinding, drying, labeling, learning. The bamboo, the fences and the water wheel happen *after* the shop work, in the afternoons and on days Arwen can spare him. By Ch31 he's a capable junior apothecary. That's why the captain takes him as a healer (Ch37), why the camp calls him "Apothecary," and why Halden eventually respects him (Ch43).

His knowledge of plants and poisons is **learned** (Arwen, Lyora, his own notes, later Halden), **not instinct**. He learns fast because he studies hard, not because it's magic. He still makes beginner's mistakes, and those can be funny.

## Esdel's personality (show it; never explain it)
- Gentle humor. He jokes to put people at ease, and never at someone's expense.
- **Careful with feelings.** He reads a person before he speaks, and he knows when to stop joking.
- Talks in a way that **comforts**: patients, children, frightened people, grumpy Arwen customers.
- **Loves to learn.** He asks "why" about every remedy and keeps a notebook in his "small, strange hand." At night he works through puzzles and sums on a slate for no reason anyone can see.
- Not a loner. He likes people and makes friends easily (Reyn, the baker, Old Richard, the children).
- His warmth sometimes draws a woman's attention. He **senses it early and steps aside kindly**, without making anyone feel foolish. He is never close with other women (the rule from *The Unspoken Summer* still holds).
- Still "the man who never complains," still quiet about himself, and his memory is still gone.

## Rules for the revision
- **Style:** *Lyrical Melancholy Realism*: short paragraphs, sensory cues (scent of crushed leaves, the pestle's sound, lamplight on jars), no bold in prose, past tense (Ch24 stays present tense).
- **Additions, not rewrites.** Keep every existing plot beat, line of dialogue that's canon in the BIBLE, and every foreshadowing line. Add roughly 150–500 words per chapter. Ch10 and Ch13 may be rebalanced more.
- **Hide the mysteries.** Nothing new about where he comes from, the woman in white, the Karunākṣa, or why he knows "physics." None of §6 Open Threads gets resolved.
- No new named characters unless needed. If one is added, log it in the BIBLE.
- **Done outside the block:** Ch72 has a callback to the Ch17 grey-leaved plant ("*Ask Arwen why.*" He never had).
- **Knock-on edits:** the BIBLE's "instinctively knows plants and poisons" becomes "learned from Arwen and Lyora in Edenridge (Ch6–31), and from Halden." Check Ch71, 72, 96 for "instinct" wording.
